- **Neural Processing Visuals**: Real-time animations showing the machine's deliberation process.

### **4. SME Control & Defensibility**
- **SME Control Layer**: Toggle regulations and adjust risk weights manually.
- **Prior Experience Module**: Factors in the SME's professional history and accuracy.
- **Blockchain Audit Trail**: Cryptographically verified history of every decision for absolute legal defensibility.
- **Persistent Learning Vault**: Saves and analyzes past cases to improve engine accuracy.

### **5. Aesthetic & UX**

## 📖 Part 2: User Manual

### **1. Getting Started**
1. **Login**: Access the portal using your SME credentials.
2. **Dashboard**: View your active cases, risk alerts, and engine accuracy stats.
3. **New Case**: Click "Create Case" to start a new analysis.

### **2. Running an Analysis**
1. **Data Entry**: Input applicant medical history, job title, and exam type.
2. **Neural Processing**: Watch the engine deliberate. You can see the "Three-Judge Panel" debating in real-time.
3. **Review EJFs**: Check the "Essential Job Functions" tab to see where medical conflicts exist.
4. **Adjust Weights**: Use the "SME Control" tab to toggle specific laws or adjust risk factor weights based on your judgment.

### **3. Generating the Report**
1. **View Dossier**: Click "Generate Executive Dossier" to view the 15+ page report.
2. **Review Verdict**: See the final consensus verdict from the Three-Judge Panel.
3. **Export**: Download the report as a professional PDF or HTML file for your records.

### **4. Using the Learning Vault**
1. **History**: Go to the "Learning Vault" to see past cases and decisions.
2. **Insights**: Review the "Similarity Analysis" to see how the engine is learning from your expertise.
3. **Accuracy**: Track your personal accuracy and the engine's performance over time.
