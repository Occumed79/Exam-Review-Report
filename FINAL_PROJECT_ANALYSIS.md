## 2. Current State Analysis: The "Liquid Glass" Platform

### 2.1 Aesthetic & UX (macOS Tahoe)
The platform now features a high-end, luminous aesthetic inspired by macOS Tahoe. 
- **Liquid Glassmorphism**: Deep blur effects and luminous borders.
- **Floating Orbs**: Dynamic, animated background elements that create depth.
- **Cursor Glow**: A light that follows the mouse, enhancing the "luminous" feel.
- **Neural Processing**: Real-time visuals showing the machine's "thinking" process.

### 2.2 Intelligence & Logic
The engine is now "nuclear-fueled" with:
- **Essential Job Functions (EJF)**: Direct mapping of medical conditions to job demands.
- **Three-Judge Panel**: A consensus-based verdict engine (Medical, Legal, Occupational).
- **Ensemble ML**: Multiple models reaching a high-precision consensus.
- **SDoH & Biometrics**: Integration of socioeconomic and real-time physiological data.

### 2.3 SME Control & Learning
- **SME Control Layer**: Allows experts to toggle regulations and adjust weights.
- **Prior Experience**: Factors in the SME's professional history and accuracy.
- **Persistent Learning Vault**: Saves every case to improve accuracy and provide historical insights.

## 3. Future Roadmap: The Path to v10.0

While the current platform is elite-tier, here are the ideas for future expansion:

### 3.1 Real-Time Telemedicine Integration
- **Feature**: Live video consultation within the dossier.
- **Impact**: Allows SMEs to interview applicants directly and update the risk score in real-time.

### 3.2 Predictive Longitudinal Modeling
- **Feature**: Simulating the applicant's health over a 10-20 year career.
- **Impact**: Identifies long-term liability and retirement/disability risk.

### 3.3 Blockchain-Verified Credentials
- **Feature**: Storing the "Audit Trail" on a private blockchain.
- **Impact**: Absolute, immutable proof of legal defensibility for high-stakes litigation.

### 3.4 AR/VR Job Simulation
- **Feature**: Integrating VR data from job simulations into the EJF engine.
- **Impact**: Provides "ground truth" data on an applicant's physical capacity in a simulated environment.
