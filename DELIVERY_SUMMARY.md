## How to Use the Expanded Engine

1. **Select Exam Type**: In the Case Intake, select the appropriate exam type (Deployment, Law Enforcement, Firefighter, DOT/FMCSA).
2. **Enter Case Data**: Provide medical conditions, injuries, and job duties as usual.
3. **View Executive Report**: Navigate to the "Executive Report" tab in the Case Hub.
4. **Analyze Compliance**: Review the Regulatory Compliance Matrix to see how the applicant stacks up against specific standards.
5. **Understand the Score**: Look at the "Intelligence Explainability" section to see the primary drivers of the risk score.
6. **Export**: Click "Export Intelligence Report" to generate a professional, one-page document for your records.
